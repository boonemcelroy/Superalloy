Superalloy @VERSION@ is installed

  • Location: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Superalloy.vst3
  • Open your DAW. If Superalloy does not appear, rescan VST3 plug-ins.
  • If you earlier copied Superalloy.vst3 into your own ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 folder, delete that copy so your DAW sees one Superalloy.
